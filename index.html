Part One

Understanding the Issue

The issue: AI automation is eating entry-level work. Under the driver of Technology, generative AI now handles the routine admin, analysis and data-entry tasks that used to be a graduate's first rung on the career ladder.
Why it matters: In the Philippines, the IT-BPM sector is a pillar of middle-class growth. If junior tasks vanish, young workers lose the chance to build skills before they are expected to have them.

Main Causes

Corporate drive to cut costs and move faster, plus enterprise software that can draft content, write code and process data.

Main Effects

  • Fewer openings for fresh graduates
  • Junior roles asking for mid-level skill
  • A fragmented career ladder
  • Cognitive deskilling from passive AI reliance

Stakeholders

  • Students and graduates: career anxiety
  • Employers (IBPAP): efficiency, analytical talent
  • Government: DOLE, DICT, TESDA, CHED
  • Office workers: displacement, wage stagnation
Part Two · The Proposed Solution

The AVE Protocol

An agency-preserving system of AI personas that turns a chatbot from a shortcut into a sparring partner. Tap a phase to hear from our guides.

Why start with you?

The Tutor withholds answers on purpose. You reason first, then the AI helps, so your thinking stays yours.

Time-flexible

Phases can shrink for a 15-minute quick draft or grow for a 2-hour deep analysis. Squeeze the time, never skip the step.

What the site keeps

Your timer, prompts, notes and scores. The AI chat itself happens in the tool you already use.

Part Two

Why It Works

AVE attacks the roots of AI deskilling: automation bias and cognitive offloading. You supply the first reasoning in the Tutor phase and defend the result in the Closing Mentor phase.
That makes you a competent AI operator: someone who can audit and defend their work under questioning. That is exactly the gap employers say fresh graduates have.

Real-world use

It needs no funding, legislation or approval. A student, freelancer or junior analyst can use it today in any AI chat: defend assumptions before text is generated, receive a critique, then do a screen-off summary before submitting to a supervisor.

Part Three

Alternatives We Weighed

1st · AVE Protocol micro level

Fastest to adopt, near-zero cost, skills transfer across tools. Rated Medium on evidence only because it has not yet been tested on a live student cohort.

2nd · Tri-Sector Apprenticeship meso level

Industry, universities and government turn entry-level jobs into paid Junior AI Operator apprenticeships. Valuable, but it depends on multi-institution coordination and risks widening the Metro Manila vs. provincial gap.

3rd · Due Process Safeguards macro level

HB 9448, Article 298 of the Labor Code and RA 11927 for human oversight and retraining. Legally grounded, but slow, and hard to enforce on foreign remote employers.

Part Four

Measuring Success

Specific

No unverified factual errors, with an auditable verification log for every assignment.

Measurable

100% completion of the closing Feynman explain-back, tracked in audit logs.

Achievable

Phase time budgets flex from 15-minute drafts to 2-hour deep dives.

Relevant

Targets the readiness gap: 37% of hiring managers report reduced entry-level hiring due to weak critical thinking.

Time-bound

Start now; evaluate improvement over 3 to 6 months.

Expected outcomes: fewer low-effort AI errors, more confidence in oral presentations and structured prompting. Long term, you keep domain literacy and digital productivity. For lasting impact, prompts stay updated across AI models and verification logs can join your digital portfolio as proof of problem-solving.
Part Five

Plan and Implementation

  1. Finalize the prompts. Write and test all six persona prompts so each stage stays consistent.
  2. Build the web app. Host the persona sequence with built-in timers.
  3. Test on real work. Run the full protocol on coursework and deliverables to find friction.
  4. Sync your notes. Export verified notes, chats and audit logs to Notion or Google Docs.

Ownership sits with the individual user and developer; student early adopters give feedback. No legal change, public budget or approval is needed. The main resource is the effort to build, host and maintain the app.

If something goes wrong

Persona drift

Tighten the prompt's role boundaries.

Build delays

Run the protocol manually in any chat window.

Rigid timing

Adjust phase durations by hand.

Deadline pressure

Compress each phase; never skip one.

Automation bias

The Feynman explain-back is the final fail-safe.
